## 3. Missing competitors (added)

- **LeadSimple:** the closest competitor to our ICP. It sits beside Buildium/AppFolio with two-way work-order sync, has owner repair approvals and shipped Maintenance AI in Jun 2026. About $265/mo at 100 doors, with a $200 floor.

- **UpKeep residential:** AI coordination, launched in Las Vegas in Jun 2026.

## 4. Product gaps found in the PRD (added)

The happy-path status machine would break on common real jobs:
1. **Diagnose, then quote.** The vendor inspects first and then asks for approval. Added `scheduled|in_progress → awaiting_quote` and `awaiting_approval → in_progress`.
2. **Emergency fast path.** Accept → `in_progress`, with approval skipped up to an emergency spend limit and the owner notified afterwards.
3. **Missed appointment / no access.** `scheduled → awaiting_scheduling`, with a reason. This is counted in reports.
4. **Invoice over the approved amount.** Over 10% blocks the close until the PM confirms. If the new total crosses the owner's threshold, the owner must approve too.
5. **Bill to:** owner, tenant (chargeback) or warranty, on the WO and the expense CSV.
6. **Trial capped at 25 doors.** That blocked the 50–300-door ICP from importing its real portfolio. Changed to full import with a 15-work-order cap.
7. Preventive maintenance and turnovers are logged as **Later** expansion paths.

## 5. Verdict

**Is the product good enough?** Yes as a wedge, but not on the original five differentiators equally.
- **The real wedge:** vendors run the whole job with no account, COI-gated dispatch, live in an afternoon, and a $99 floor (under LeadSimple's $200 and Property Meld's $160).
- **Table stakes:** clocks, nudges and owner approval links must be done well, but they won't win deals against Meld or LeadSimple on their own.
- **The window is closing:** AppFolio, Buildium and LeadSimple all shipped maintenance AI in 2026. Speed to design partners matters more than feature breadth.

**Must-validate before building past week 4:**
- **E1:** do Buildium prospects pay for Lumina Workforce? Did they try LeadSimple or Property Meld, and why did they stop?
- **E4:** do vendors complete accept/decline through a link without calling the PM? This is the whole wedge. If it fails, the product has no edge.
